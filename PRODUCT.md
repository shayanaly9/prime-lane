# PRODUCT.md

## Overview
PrimeLane Logistics is a premium freight and logistics provider specializing in container shipping, vehicle transport, and industrial freight, with a particular focus on the Australia-Dubai corridor.

## Product Purpose
To provide businesses with a reliable, transparent, and high-end logistics experience for international and domestic freight.

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional**: Authoritative in logistics expertise.
- **Premium**: High-end service feel, avoiding "budget" aesthetics.
- **Reliable**: Secure and trustworthy.
- **Industrial**: Rooted in physical transport but technically modern.

## Users
- B2B exporters and importers.
- Vehicle owners moving cars internationally.
- Industrial firms requiring DG-certified transport.

## Register
brand

## Strategic Principles
- **Directness**: Highlighting the direct corridor to Dubai.
- **Clarity**: Simple navigation through complex logistics services.
- **Visual Depth**: Using premium design elements (grain, reveals) to differentiate from competitors.
